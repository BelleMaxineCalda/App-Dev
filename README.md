### The Nun (2018)

**The Nun (2018)** is a supernatural horror film set in 1952 and acts as a prequel to *The Conjuring* The movie is about **Sister Irene**, a young novice nun, who is sent by the Vatican to investigate the mysterious suicide of a nun at a remote abbey in Romania. She is accompanied by Father Burke, a priest with a troubled past. As they uncover the abbey's dark secrets, they discover that it is haunted by **Valak**, a powerful demon introduced in *The Conjuring 2*. The duo must work together to stop the demon's influence and prevent it from escaping the abbey and causing widespread terror.
